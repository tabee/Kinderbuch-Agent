# Character Bible — The Thing That Wears My Father's Face

## Fritz (Protagonist; a nineteen-year-old grieving man who breaks his village's oldest law to search for his lost father in the Schattenrachen)

A quiet, broken young man haunted by the death of his mother and disappearance of his father. He lives on the village's crooked edge in profound, invisible grief. In the forest, he is poisoned, torn open, and remade by the ancient thing's hunger—yet refuses its final seduction and burns it from within.

**Visual keywords:** Lean frame weathered by grief and village isolation, Mud-stained, tattered clothes torn from running through root-bones, Pale exhausted face marked by faded scratches and worn patches, Hollow eyes reflecting the greasy grey light of the Schattenrachen

**Primary reference:** `references/fritz.png`

## Shadow (Fritz's only companion; a loyal black dog who leads Fritz into the Schattenrachen and awaits his return)

A scarred black dog bonded to Fritz, carrying marks of violence from a forgotten past. Shadow is the catalyst for Fritz's descent into the forest and his hope for return.

**Visual keywords:** Solid black fur, Visible scars and white lines crisscrossing the coat, Medium-large dog frame, Alert, forward-facing posture

**Primary reference:** `references/shadow.png`

## The Shadowflayer (One of the ancient thing's appendages; a creature of branches and smoke that strips Fritz of his shadow as a form of violation and consumption)

A tall, impossible thing composed of branches and smoke with a smooth dark face and joints that bend in geometrically wrong directions. It moves with reverent precision, peeling away the shadow from Fritz's body with terrible gentleness.

**Visual keywords:** Tall, skeletal frame of twisted branches, Smooth dark face like polished obsidian, Wisping smoke curling around the limbs, Joints bending backward and sideways in unnatural angles, Cavity or hollow in the chest for storing stolen shadows

**Primary reference:** `references/the-shadowflayer.png`

## The Fearcage (One of the ancient thing's appendages; a ribcage of yellowed bone bound by black ligament that locks onto Fritz and forces his worst memories into endless loops)

A flattened, yellowed ribcage held together with black sinew and ligament. It is a cage designed to break bodies and minds simultaneously by forcing traumatic memories to repeat while the structure tightens mercilessly around its prey.

**Visual keywords:** Yellowed, aged bone bleached with time, Black ligaments and sinew threading through the ribs, Rib-like cage structure, elongated horizontally, Teeth-like sound when it locks shut, Crusted, organic texture suggesting age and absorbed suffering

**Primary reference:** `references/the-fearcage.png`

## The False Father (The ancient thing's final appendage; a humanoid manifestation wearing the face, voice, and gestures of Fritz's dead father, designed to seduce Fritz into willingly accepting consumption)

A perfect mimic of Fritz's father—bearing his exact face, tired eyes, and familiar hand shapes. It speaks with gentled sorrow and offers comfort, but is merely the forest's tongue given human form to manipulate a dying son into acceptance and consent.

**Visual keywords:** Middle-aged man's face identical to Fritz's father, Tired, weathered eyes conveying false sorrow, Weathered hands with familiar gestures, Ordinary worn travelling clothes as if returning home, Slight unnatural stillness in movement—too controlled, too precise

**Primary reference:** `references/the-false-father.png`

## The Schattenrachen (The antagonist; an ancient, sentient creature that fell from lightless depths and has waited patiently for millennia, consuming all who enter its mouth, which appears as a forest)

Not a forest but the living mouth of an ancient, immobile thing. Its teeth are trees, its breath is mist, its hunger is patient and vast. It digests everything—shadow, memory, name, soul—keeping prey alive and aware through appendages like the Shadowflayer and Fearcage. It speaks through thousands of trapped voices and moves with the slowness of geological time.

**Visual keywords:** Crooked, twisted trees forming tooth-like formations, Greasy grey mist filling the air like breath, Wrong geometry and paths that seal themselves with sudden growth, Root-bones tangling the ground in a nest-like pattern, Dark, wet throat-like center pulsing in the forest's deepest point

**Primary reference:** `references/the-schattenrachen.png`
