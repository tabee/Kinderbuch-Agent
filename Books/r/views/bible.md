# Character Bible — Die Legende von Rübenzahl (The Turnip Counter)

## Princess Emma (The adventurous protagonist who discovers the spirit's palace and escapes through cleverness)

A young, brave princess who ventures into misty mountains seeking adventure. She is kind-hearted and resourceful, using her wit to outsmart the spirit and regain her freedom. Emma is curious, observant, and discovers the spirit's weakness for counting and order.

**Visual keywords:** Long golden blonde hair, Pink silk dress with white lace trim, Small golden crown with jewels, White leather boots

**Primary reference:** `references/princess-emma.png`

## Rübezahl (The Spirit) (The lonely, magical spirit who lives underground and becomes obsessed with counting turnips)

A tall, shadowy figure with kind but deeply sad and lonely eyes. He dwells in the dark stone palace beneath the earth and is driven by an compulsive need to count and organize. His form is ethereal and mystical, glowing faintly with magical energy. Over time, he becomes increasingly tired and thin from endless counting.

**Visual keywords:** Tall shadowy form with wispy edges, Large, sorrowful luminous eyes, Dark grey or charcoal robes, Glowing pale blue aura around body

**Primary reference:** `references/rubezahl-the-spirit.png`

## The Star-Eyed Horse (The magical escape companion created from the enchanted final turnip)

A beautiful black horse conjured from the last turnip through Emma's secret spell. It has eyes that shimmer like stars and serves as Emma's means of escape from the underground palace back to the sunlit world above.

**Visual keywords:** Glossy jet-black coat, Eyes that sparkle like white stars, Flowing dark mane, Graceful, elegant legs
