# Die Legende von Rübenzahl (The Turnip Counter)

**Premise:** When a lonely, obsessive mountain spirit lures Princess Emma to a cursed waterfall, he imprisons her in his shadowy palace beneath the Giant Mountains. To manipulate her despair, he conjures magical turnips that briefly transform into her friends—only to wither into ash, deepening her anguish. Desperate to escape, Emma exploits his dark compulsion to count, challenging him to tally an infinite field of turnips. As the spirit descends into manic obsession, losing himself in the endless rows, Emma performs a final, forbidden spell that transforms the last turnip into a black stallion wreathed in shadow. She flees into the darkness, but the spirit's curse follows her forever—he is condemned to eternally count turnips in the depths, known as Rübezahl, the Turnip Counter, a warning of greed and obsession that devours the soul.
