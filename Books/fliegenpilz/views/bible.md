# Character Bible — Fliegenpilz - Der Traum vom Fliegen

## Flo (Protagonist – a young fly agaric mushroom who dreams of flying)

Flo is a small, cheerful red fly agaric mushroom with a gentle spirit. He spends his days observing the world from his forest spot, yearning to soar through the sky. Through his journey with the Shaman, he discovers that the greatest magic lies not in flying itself, but in friendship, wonder, and the beauty of the world seen from new perspectives.

**Visual keywords:** bright red cap with white polka dots, small cream-coloured stem, tiny dark eyes gazing upward, roots anchored in earth below

**Primary reference:** `references/flo.png`

## Die Waldschamanin (Guide and wisdom-keeper – an elderly forest shaman who opens Flo's world)

The Forest Shaman is a wise, gentle elder who possesses the magic to help Flo experience flight. With a kind smile and mystical knowledge of the forest's secrets, she recognizes the dreams within the small mushroom and becomes his guide on an unforgettable journey. She represents wisdom, compassion, and the transformative power of sharing wonder.

**Visual keywords:** long flowing robes in earthy browns and greens, ornately decorated wicker basket with mysterious symbols, silver or grey hair flowing loose, weathered hands that move with intention

**Primary reference:** `references/die-waldschamanin.png`

## Der Fuchs (Wise messenger – a clever fox who shares knowledge of the forest's nocturnal magic)

An intelligent and graceful fox who encounters the Shaman and Flo during their journey. With gleaming, knowing eyes, he speaks of night wanderings, hidden paths, and the subtle magic woven throughout the forest. He represents the wisdom found in quiet observation and the hidden wonders that exist beyond the visible world.

**Visual keywords:** russet-orange fur with deep auburn accents, bright, glimmering eyes full of intelligence, bushy, full tail held with quiet dignity, alert pointed ears

**Primary reference:** `references/der-fuchs.png`

## Der Hase (Courageous friend – a brave rabbit who teaches that small creatures can achieve great things)

A spirited and courageous young hare who springs forth from the greenery with boundless energy. Despite his small size, he has explored the vast world and lived wonderful adventures. Through his conversations with the Shaman, he reveals to Flo that size does not limit dreams, and that brave hearts accomplish extraordinary things. He embodies courage, curiosity, and the power of small steps.

**Visual keywords:** soft grey-brown fur with lighter cream belly, long, alert ears with pink inner flesh, strong hind legs ready for a leap, bright, hopeful dark eyes

**Primary reference:** `references/der-hase.png`
